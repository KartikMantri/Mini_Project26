# Phase 5 variant — 2:1 Long-Short (100 long / 50 short), top 5% long / bottom 5% short

Generated by [`backtest_finbert_2to1.py`](../backtest_finbert_2to1.py) `--long-pct 0.05` `--short-pct 0.05`. The main market-neutral result is unchanged: [`Phase5_finbert_results.md`](Phase5_finbert_results.md).

**Picks:** at each call, LONG if its score is in the top 5% of the previous 365 days' scores, SHORT if in the bottom 5%; enter at day +2's close, exit at day +60's.  
**Sizing:** per 100 of capital, 100 long and 50 short (equal weight within each side), so the strategy is net 50% long.  
**Costs:** 10 bps per buy or sell on the weight traded; 0.5% a year to borrow the 50 short.  
**Returns are raw** (they include the market's moves). *vs S&P 500* = the strategy's annual return minus the S&P 500's, with a Newey-West t-test (10 lags) on the daily differences.

## Notes

- Holdout without 2020: +9.8% a year, Sharpe 0.64, vs S&P 500 -1.1% a year (p 0.822).
- **Dividends:** stock prices are dividend-adjusted but the S&P 500 series is the price index, so *vs S&P 500* flatters the strategy by roughly the index's dividend yield (about 1.5–2% a year over this period).
- Average positions open (holdout): 9 long, 9 short.
- Same caveats as the main backtest: survivorship bias, closing-price fills, no cost for daily rebalancing.
- **The 5% long / 5% short cut-offs were chosen after seeing earlier results**, so this run is an exploratory variant, not a pre-set test like the main result.
- Series end on 2025-05-08, the last new trade under these picks; the S&P 500 is cut to the same dates.
