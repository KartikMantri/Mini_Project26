# Phase 5 variant — Long-only, top 15% of scores

Generated by [`backtest_finbert_longonly.py`](../backtest_finbert_longonly.py). The main market-neutral result is unchanged: [`Phase5_finbert_results.md`](Phase5_finbert_results.md).

**Rule:** at each call, BUY if its score is in the top 15% of the previous 365 days' scores; enter at day +2's close, exit at day +60's. 100% of capital, equal weight across open positions, no shorting; if nothing is open the money sits in the S&P 500.  
**Costs:** 10 bps per buy or sell on the weight traded.  
**Returns are raw.** *vs S&P 500* = annual return minus the S&P 500's (Newey-West t, 10 lags). *Buy every call* = same timing and weights, every call bought, no score: the fair yardstick for what the score adds.

## Notes

- Holdout without 2020: +16.1% a year, Sharpe 0.88.
- Average positions open (holdout): 27. Weight traded: 9.5× capital per year.
- **Dividends:** stock prices are dividend-adjusted but the S&P 500 series is the price index, so *vs S&P 500* flatters the strategy by roughly the index's dividend yield (about 1.5–2% a year). *Buy every call* has the same treatment, so the score's contribution is measured fairly against it.
- **The 15% cut-off was chosen after seeing the earlier runs**, so this is an exploratory variant, not a pre-set test like the main result.
- Same caveats as the main backtest: survivorship bias, closing-price fills, no cost for daily rebalancing.
- Series end on 2025-05-14, the last new trade; the S&P 500 is cut to the same dates.
