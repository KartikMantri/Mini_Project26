# Phase 5 (v2) — Trading Backtest on the FinBERT score

Generated by [`backtest_finbert.py`](../backtest_finbert.py). Method: [`Phase5_Trading_Backtest.md`](Phase5_Trading_Backtest.md).

**Rule:** at each call, LONG if its score is in the top 20% of the scores of all calls in the previous 365 days, SHORT if in the bottom 20%. Enter at the close of day +2, exit at the close of day +60. Equal weight within each leg, long leg minus short leg every day.  
**Costs:** 10 bps per buy or sell, 0.5% a year to borrow the short leg.  
**Returns** are daily, annualised as mean × 252; volatility = daily std × √252; Sharpe = annual return / volatility; t from Newey-West (10 lags) on daily returns. The S&P 500 is the price index (no dividends).

## Notes

- No setting was tuned on the backtest: the 20% cut-off and the day +2 → +60 hold come from Phase 4, costs are assumed.
- Scores come from Phase 3's walk-forward models, so every score was made without seeing that call's future; the cut-offs use only earlier calls.
- Universe: today's 200 tickers, so firms that disappeared before 2025 are missing (survivorship bias). PARA is excluded (bad prices, see `phase1_abnormal_returns.py`).
- Trades are filled at the closing price with no market impact; daily rebalancing between equal weights is not charged.
